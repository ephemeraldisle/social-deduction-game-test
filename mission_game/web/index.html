A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

One table. Two teams.
Four missions to win.

1. Make a proposal. The chairman picks a crew. Each crew member makes a public pledge; everyone votes. Five Yes votes approve. After the full vote, everyone receives one token, pass or fail.

2. Commit in secret. The crew spends tokens. A contribution can differ from its pledge. Only the final pot and wallets are made public.

3. Read the result. Reach the mission's funding target to score. Blue beats Red on a tie. Green funds the mission but cannot own it.

4. Hear the table. Players report what happened. These are claims, and may be false. Everyone gets one token if the game continues.

Read your private objective: it may add a condition to winning with your team or explicitly change which team you want to win. New tables deal private objectives and hidden abilities. Read your own ability for its timing and controls. Contrarian is disabled. Earlier saves keep their original rules. Computer players use scripted policies and can make imperfect decisions.
